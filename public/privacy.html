Evidence privacy notice

When you submit evidence, civicAId sends your text and any attached file to Google Gemini for the limited purpose of producing an advisory assessment. A human reviewer makes the final award decision.

civicAId does not retain the raw evidence after the request. It keeps a submission identifier, your user identifier, the challenge, timestamps, the model name, a short non-sensitive reason, and the final decision for security and audit purposes. Provider handling is governed by the deployment's Google service terms and retention settings.

Before submitting, remove information that is not needed: faces, home addresses, signatures, document metadata, voter details, and information about children or other people. Supported images have metadata removed before provider transmission.

Deletion and questions

Contact the organization operating this deployment using its published privacy contact to request deletion of retained audit records or to ask how its Google account is configured. Do not submit evidence until that contact and the deployment-specific retention period have been published.
